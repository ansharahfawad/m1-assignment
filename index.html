Getting started

How the Adoption Process Works

  1. Explore: Read about the available animals.
  2. Ask questions: Learn about the pet's health and needs.
  3. Prepare: Make sure your home and budget are ready.
  4. Welcome them: Provide care, patience, and affection.

Plan ahead

Things to Consider Before Adopting

Adoption is a long-term commitment. Consider these responsibilities first.

Responsibility What to Consider
Time Daily feeding, exercise, training, and companionship.
Health Vaccinations, checkups, and veterinary expenses.
Home A safe space that suits the pet's needs.
Commitment Caring for the animal throughout its life.
