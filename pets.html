Before your visit

Prepare for Adoption

Preparing questions ahead of time can help you make a responsible decision.

  • Ask about the pet's health and vaccinations.
  • Discuss food, exercise, and training needs.
  • Tell the shelter about other pets at home.
  • Prepare a safe and quiet space for your pet.

Learn more from Adopt a Pet or visit SNIPSA .
